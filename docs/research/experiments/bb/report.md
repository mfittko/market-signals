# BB: Bollinger band width and band walks under E0 costs

This report claims no edge and no profitable strategy. Its only conclusions are the preregistered decisions below. It is generated by `platform/cmd/research-bb` from the frozen spec and the exports that the tables pin by sha256.

Questions:

1. Does Bollinger band width at entry separate cheap trades from expensive ones under the E0 rules?
2. Does a band walk (N bid closes in a row beyond the outer band) predict continuation, measured as net R after cost against random entries, and does it do better when it starts from a squeeze?

## Rules

The rules are preregistered in the [BB spec](spec.json). Each instrument copies the [E0 spec](../e0/spec.json) and changes only its instrument and dataset: per instrument, the runner copies the parsed E0 spec and overrides only its instrument name, quote currency and dataset path and sha256; the feed, the E0 strategy, the spread rule, the decision delay, the financing basis, the markup and the 17:00 America/New_York rollover apply to every instrument unchanged. Question 1 scores the E0 trades (Supertrend 10/3, 1.5 ATR stop, 2 R target, reversal, 200-bar time stop). Question 2 enters on band walks with the stop at the middle band, a 2 R target, a 200-bar time stop and no reversal exit. Both use the E0 fills, spread and financing.
